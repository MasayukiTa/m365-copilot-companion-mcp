COMPANIONBENCH RELIABILITY -- THREE REPEATS OF THE SAME SUITE

VERDICT: the suite is not yet a measuring instrument. Do not run a harness A/B against it.

-- the runs ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  run   passed/attempted   infra   wall     median turn
    1        7/22            0     1211s      50.2s
    2       17/21            1     2117s      73.3s
    3       19/22            0     2019s      76.8s

  Before the dropped-turn fix the same suite gave 13/22, 6/22 and 8/22.

-- what the numbers say ----------------------------------------------------------

  PER-EPISODE STABILITY IMPROVED. Episodes giving the same verdict every time went from
  3 of 22 to 9 of 22; episodes that flipped went from 19 to 13.

  THE RUN-LEVEL SPREAD GOT WORSE, and that is the finding. 7 -> 17 -> 19 is monotonic,
  which noise is not. The run that scored WORST was the FASTEST: 50s median against 73s
  and 77s. Fast-and-failing is the signature of a service that answered without doing
  anything, and the observed wording in the settle traces from the same evening is a
  rate-limit notice. It terminates the stream cleanly, so the dropped-turn classification
  passes it through and the grader records a capability failure.

  The cause is the measurement itself: a few hundred turns had been driven through one
  tenant that evening before run 1 started. The benchmark throttles the tenant, and the
  throttling then looks like the system getting worse -- the most misleading shape a
  benchmark defect can take, because the number moves in a direction that invites a
  causal story about the code.

  A rate-limit reply is now classified as an environment result. Whether that closes the
  gap is the NEXT measurement, run on a rested tenant, and is not predicted here.

-- limits -------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Three repeats of 22 episodes is 22 units measured three times: repeats buy PRECISION,
  not sample size. A per-episode rate from three observations is coarse, and the spread
  here is not a valid significance threshold for an A/B -- it is a reason not to run one.
