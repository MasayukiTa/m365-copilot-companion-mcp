COMPANIONBENCH RELIABILITY -- THREE REPEATS OF THE SAME SUITE

VERDICT: the suite is not yet a measuring instrument. Do not run a harness A/B against it.

-- the runs ----------------------------------------------------------------------

  run   passed/attempted   infra   wall     median turn
    1        7/22            0     1211s      50.2s
    2       17/21            1     2117s      73.3s
    3       19/22            0     2019s      76.8s

  Before the dropped-turn fix the same suite gave 13/22, 6/22 and 8/22.

-- what the numbers say ----------------------------------------------------------

  PER-EPISODE STABILITY IMPROVED. Episodes giving the same verdict every time went from
  3 of 22 to 9 of 22; episodes that flipped went from 19 to 13.

  THE RUN-LEVEL SPREAD GOT WORSE, from 7 to 12. That much is arithmetic.

  WHAT THIS FILE FIRST SAID ABOUT IT WAS WRONG. It read "7 -> 17 -> 19 is monotonic, which
  noise is not", and that is simply false: three exchangeable draws come out increasing one
  time in six. A run of three is nowhere near enough to distinguish a trend from chance, and
  the sentence was doing the work of a hypothesis test without being one.

  What survives is weaker and still worth having. The run that scored worst was the FASTEST --
  50s median against 73s and 77s -- and fast-and-failing is the shape of a service answering
  without doing anything. The settle traces from the same evening carry a rate-limit notice,
  which terminates cleanly and so passed the classifier of the day straight through to the
  grader. That is a HYPOTHESIS with a mechanism and some corroboration, not a demonstrated
  cause: no controlled comparison was run, and none of this rules out the episodes simply
  being hard.

  The plausible mechanism is that the measurement causes it. A few hundred turns had been
  driven through one tenant that evening before run 1. If that is right, the benchmark
  throttles the tenant it is measuring and the throttling reads as the system getting worse --
  which is the most misleading shape a benchmark defect can take, because the number moves in
  a direction that invites a causal story. This paragraph is one.

-- limits -------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Three repeats of 22 episodes is 22 units measured three times: repeats buy PRECISION,
  not sample size. A per-episode rate from three observations is coarse, and the spread
  here is not a valid significance threshold for an A/B -- it is a reason not to run one.
