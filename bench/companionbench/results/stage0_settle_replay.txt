SETTLE UNIFICATION -- STAGE 0 (SECOND COLLECTION, DECISIVE)

VERDICT: the proposed change is not justified by this evidence. Do not proceed to the
implementation stages. The one real early-accept found came from somewhere else and is
already fixed.

-- the collection ----------------------------------------------------------------

  120 turns, 12 prompts x 10 cycles, driven through the real wait_for_idle on a live
  tenant, recorded into this campaign's own file. Zero probe contamination.

  Label tail: 15 polls, about 8 seconds past acceptance -- comfortably beyond the dwell
  production itself requires. The first collection used 4 polls (~2s) and every one of its
  127 tails was exactly 4 long with no change, which left the result unreadable: a zero
  could have meant the predicate was fine or that nobody looked long enough.

-- the result --------------------------------------------------------------------

  TURNS WHOSE TEXT CHANGED AFTER PRODUCTION ACCEPTED: 0 of 120, at 8 seconds.

  That is a direct observation of the phenomenon, not an inference from a rate. Production
  accepted, and the answer then sat unchanged for eight seconds, on every turn.

settle replay: 120 turns
  legacy   accepted  120  never   0  truncated    0  rate 0.000
  sampled  accepted  120  never   0  truncated    0  rate 0.000
  reduction:  0 of 120 turns no longer truncated  =  0.000  95% CI [0.000, 0.250]
              interval method: cluster bootstrap over 12 prompt(s)
  discordant: legacy worse 0 / sampled worse 0
  (sampled_worse is structurally zero: the sampled predicate is the legacy one plus a floor, so it cannot accept earlier. This is not evidence -- it is the definition.)

  The interval is over 12 clusters, not 120 turns: twelve prompts cycled ten times are
  twelve independent units. With no events observed the bound is the rule of three over
  those units, so 0.250 rather than the 0.030 a turn-level Wilson would print. A
  nonparametric bootstrap would give [0, 0] here, which claims certainty a bootstrap
  cannot have about an event it never saw.

-- what this does and does not settle ---------------------------------------------

  SETTLED: the plan's ~7% truncated-capture figure is not reproduced on this population,
  and the sampled predicate has nothing to fix here. Its sample floor never changed an
  acceptance to a different text.

  NOT SETTLED, and this is the limit that matters: the prompts are ordinary chat questions.
  They stream steadily. The turns most at risk of an early accept are the ones with PAUSES
  -- a tool call, a retrieval, a long think -- and this population does not contain them.
  A convenience sample of one tenant on one night may therefore exclude precisely the turns
  the plan was written about. Reproducing it with agent/tool turns is the collection that
  would close this, and it is not the same run.

-- what WAS found ----------------------------------------------------------------

  In the first collection, one genuine early accept: on a throttled request Copilot showed
  a 'still organising the information' placeholder and production accepted it as the
  answer, because that string was not in PROCESSING_MARKERS. No sample floor prevents that
  -- a placeholder is perfectly stable for as many samples as you require. It is now a
  marker. The defect was in the marker table, not in the predicate the plan proposes to
  replace.
