VERDICT
  BOUNDED, NOT ABSENT. Within this corpus neither rule truncated a turn whose
  stream went quiet for up to about 26 seconds. Above that the corpus is silent,
  and silence is not a zero. So the primary endpoint is answered for short pauses
  and open for long ones -- which is the interesting half, since the failure this
  replaces was described as a pause that outlasted the dwell.


  WHY THEY AGREED, AND IT IS NOT SAMPLE SIZE.
    6 samples at a measured 0.66s poll interval  ->  3.3s
    the dwell itself                             ->  4.0s
    binding constraint: DWELL

  The sample requirement is satisfied BEFORE the dwell is, so at these
  settings it can never be the thing that delays an accept. The two rules
  are identical by arithmetic, not by luck -- 0 discordant pairs is
  STRUCTURAL, and no number of extra clusters would produce one.

  So Stage 1 would measure nothing here. That is not a reason to drop the
  unified rule: it buys one rule in one place instead of four, and the
  guard starts binding the moment the interval grows past 0.80s -- a slow
  tenant or a loaded machine, which is exactly when a stream pauses and
  truncation gets likely. It IS a reason not to claim it as an improvement.
